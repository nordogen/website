# Approved product declarations — regulatory source of truth

Extracted verbatim from `data/tekstovi deklaracija/*.docx` (the client's approved outer-packaging
texts) on 2026-07-28. The `.docx` originals are gitignored local-only source material; this file
is the tracked, greppable copy.

**This wording is binding.** Any medical, ingredient, dosage, population or warning text that
appears on the website is copied verbatim from here — never paraphrased, "improved" or softened.
See `AGENTS.md` § Copy rules.

Serbian only. There is no approved English translation; an unofficial one carries liability, which
is why product pages show a marketing summary in English rather than a translated declaration.

Note **URINORD is not a food supplement** — it is *dijetetski proizvod / hrana za posebne medicinske
namene* (food for special medical purposes) and requires a medical-supervision notice. It cannot be
presented identically to the other five.
